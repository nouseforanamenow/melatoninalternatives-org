Core Argument: Why Melatonin Fails

Dosing Problem: Commercial melatonin (3-10mg) exceeds physiological dose (0.3-0.5mg) by 6-20x. Higher doses accelerate receptor desensitization and suppress natural production.

Quality Problem: 71% of melatonin supplements don't contain labeled amounts. Actual content ranges from 83% less to 478% more than labeled. (Erland & Bhd, 2017, JCSM)

Tolerance Problem: MT1/MT2 receptor downregulation with regular use. Rebound insomnia when discontinued. Long-term suppression of pineal gland's natural melatonin production.

Pathway Problem: Melatonin signals "time to sleep" (circadian timing) but doesn't create sleep pressure (adenosine). Adenosine-pathway supplements like Reishi create actual sleep drive without tolerance.

Melatonin Transition Protocol (Summary)

  1. Weeks 1-2: Reduce melatonin dose by 50%. Introduce magnesium glycinate (300mg before bed).
  2. Weeks 3-4: Continue reduced melatonin. Add Reishi (1,500mg) or L-theanine (200mg).
  3. Weeks 5-6: Reduce melatonin to 0.3mg every other night. Maintain alternatives daily.
  4. Weeks 7-8: Discontinue melatonin. Continue alternatives. Some temporary sleep disruption is normal.
