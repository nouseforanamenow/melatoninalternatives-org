How to Quit Melatonin Safely: A 4-Week Transition Protocol

Quitting melatonin without a plan triggers 1-3 weeks of rebound insomnia—often the worst sleep you've ever experienced. Your brain craves the chemical it's been receiving nightly. Withdrawal is real, painful, and why most people resume melatonin.

This guide provides a structured 4-week transition protocol that overlaps melatonin with natural alternatives, gradually weaning off melatonin while building sustainable sleep support. Follow this protocol to minimize rebound insomnia, establish new sleep pathways, and achieve melatonin-independent sleep.
